Help & Information

Rental Requirements & FAQ

Valid Driver's License

Must hold a valid national license or International Driving Permit (IDP).

Identification

Valid passport or official government-issued photo ID required upon collection.

Age Requirement

Minimum driver age is 21 years (additional surcharges may apply under 25).

Security Deposit

A refundable security deposit pre-authorization is required at vehicle pickup.

How can I book a car?
You can easily request a vehicle online using our booking form or directly reach out via our official WhatsApp connection.
What documents do I need?
You will need your original valid driving license, passport/national ID card, and a valid credit or debit card for the deposit.
What is the minimum rental period?
Our minimum rental period is 24 hours (1 full day). Partial-day rentals are charged at the full daily rate.
Do you offer airport pickup?
Yes, we provide 24/7 airport pickup and drop-off options at specified terminal locations.
Is insurance included?
Basic standard third-party coverage is included in all rentals. Comprehensive protection packages can be added optionally.
